01 / about me

More than
a résumé.

I’m an aerospace engineer who likes to move between disciplines — from propulsion and systems engineering to AI, music and creative technology.

I’m fascinated by how complex things work, how individual decisions become system-level behaviour, and how an idea becomes something real. I learn by going down rabbit holes, building, breaking, questioning and trying again.

This website is the living version of that process. It is not only a list of qualifications. It is a record of what I’m learning, what I’m building and where my curiosity is taking me.
